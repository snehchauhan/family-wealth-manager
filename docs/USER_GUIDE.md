# User guide

## Navigation
Sidebar: **Dashboard · Transactions · Accounts · Investments · Assets & Liabilities ·
Net Worth · Reports · Settings**. Top bar: family-member filter (Father / Mother /
Household / all), dark-mode toggle, and a **Quick Add** button for income, expense,
transfer, investment transaction, asset, or liability.

## Dashboard
Pick any month (dropdown) or "Whole year", plus the year. All cards, charts and the savings
rate update instantly: income vs expenses, monthly savings, net worth over time, expense
category pie, asset allocation pie (by investment type), and net-worth composition lines
(bank / investments / assets / liabilities). YTD figures follow the current calendar year.

## Transactions
Filters: type, date range (from/to), free-text search over description/notes/subcategory/
beneficiary, family member (top bar), pagination, CSV export. Every row can be edited or
deleted (with confirmation). Duplicate-looking entries (same type/date/amount/accounts)
trigger a warning you can override with "Save anyway".

## Accounts
Add savings/current/FD/cash accounts (name, bank, owner, opening balance + date — never
full account numbers or credentials). Click an account for: current balance, month-end
balance history chart, and full account transaction list. Transfers between accounts are
made from Quick Add or Transactions; they never touch income/expense totals.

## Investments
Add any investment (mutual fund, stock, FD, gold, bonds, property, custom). Inside an
investment's detail you record dated transactions: purchase/SIP (amount, units, NAV),
redemptions, dividends, switches. Click the *Current value* cell (or edit the investment)
to record today's market value — doing this monthly gives exact history. The list shows
contributed, withdrawn, current value, gain, return %, and XIRR ("—" when data is
insufficient). FDs show maturity dates. The SIP tracker compares planned SIPs with the
installments you actually recorded — nothing is assumed paid.

## Assets & Liabilities / Net Worth
Assets: purchase price vs current estimated value with appreciation. Liabilities: lender,
outstanding, EMI, rate. Net Worth page: current snapshot, composition pie, and a month-end
history table from your first transaction, flagging any month estimated at cost.

## Reports / Backup
Year-filtered CSV exports (monthly, yearly, transactions, investment performance, accounts,
net worth), full Excel workbook (every table, one sheet each), full JSON backup, restore
with confirmation, and a print-ready annual report (browser Print → Save as PDF).

## Conventions
- Amounts displayed with Indian grouping (₹1,00,000.00)
- Dates displayed DD-MM-YYYY; date inputs use your browser's picker
